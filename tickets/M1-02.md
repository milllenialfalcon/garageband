## What and why
A downloadable Android app the founder installs directly on his phone, with nothing built on his PC.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Manually triggered workflow builds an installable Android APK (local Gradle build on ubuntu, no EAS account needed) and uploads it as an artifact
- AC2: Signing: a debug or internal signing key for now; the production signing key is a needs-you item (losing it would block future updates, so the founder must hold it)
- AC3: docs/fork/mobile.md: how to download and install the APK and pair it with an Enigma server by QR code over Wi-Fi or Tailscale
